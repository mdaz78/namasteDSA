Time & Space Complexity

A guided course on Big-O, built to one mission: state and justify the complexity of a solution in a coding interview, and compare approaches under pressure.

Why this course exists. In interviews you want to state the time and space complexity of your solution out loud, justify it in a sentence or two, and compare a brute-force approach against an optimised one — without guessing. Success looks like: deriving Big-O by counting loops and recursive calls and getting it right first try; explaining why constants and lower-order terms are dropped; ranking the common classes and naming an algorithm for each; and answering "can you do better?" by finding the bottleneck.

Working with your teacher

These lessons are half the loop. The other half is asking the agent in your Claude Code session to explain anything unclear, analyse your own .js solutions from this repo, or quiz you live. That feedback loop is what makes it stick.

The steering files — MISSION.md, RESOURCES.md, GLOSSARY.md, NOTES.md, learning-records/ — are the machine-readable source of truth your teacher reads between sessions. The pages linked above are the readable versions of the same content.
